ArchGX - third-party notices
============================

This file goes with every ArchGX release file: the installer apps
(ArchGX-Installer-*), archgx-<version>.tar.gz and the console payload inside them.
Full licence texts are in the LICENSES/ folder of the release archive and of
https://github.com/ArchGX/ArchGX. Source for every GPL component is attached to
each release (see SOURCE.txt) and is in the repository at the release tag.
